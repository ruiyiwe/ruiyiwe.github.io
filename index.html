Deep 3D-aware sketch generation with multi-view consistency and style transfer

2026

Honours thesis, supervised by Dr. Fanglue Zhang and Dr. Junhong Zhao

In progress

Existing sketch generation methods work in 2D, so the same subject drawn from two angles rarely looks like the same drawing. I represent a sketch as a set of 3D Bézier strokes and optimise them jointly across several viewpoints with differentiable rendering, so one underlying geometry explains every view. Perceptual and semantic objectives (LPIPS and CLIP-based losses) drive visual quality and style.

Object-based DETR-like model for flood area segmentation

2025

Course project, supervised by Prof. Bastiaan Kleijn. Code

Pixel-level segmentation models tend to blur flood boundaries and miss scattered, irregular water regions. I adapted DETR's object queries for dense prediction: a ResNet-18 backbone, a lightweight Transformer encoder-decoder, and a redesigned mask head that builds per-class masks directly from the learned queries. Trained and compared with a ViT baseline on FloodNet under identical conditions.

0.539 mIoU against 0.555 for ViT: slightly lower overall, with visibly sharper boundaries between flood regions.

Selection operators in genetic programming

2025

Summer Research Scholarship, supervised by Prof. Bing Xue, Prof. Mengjie Zhang and Dr. Qi Chen

Compared ε-lexicase, down-sampled tournament and Pareto tournament selection on synthetic (Friedman 1–3) and real-world symbolic regression benchmarks. Down-sampled tournaments favour exploitation, ε-lexicase preserves rare solution behaviours, and Pareto tournaments favour simpler, more efficient programs, which gives practical guidance for choosing a selection method from dataset characteristics.
